Status: Active concise coding-agent context. Read canonical docs before implementing.

## Direction
Luxury High-Fashion Wedding Editorial.
Core principle: INTRICATE DETAILS, RESTRAINED COMPOSITION.
Premium wedding stationery brought to life digitally; elegant, refined, romantic, modern, spacious, editorial.

Avoid generic wedding templates, SaaS/dashboard UI, scrapbook styling, childish/cute styling, excessive vintage treatment, excessive floral decoration, overly pink aesthetics, heavy gradients/shadows, and excessive animation.

## Opening — locked
Editorial Cover focused on the couple monogram, `RIZAL & DEWI`, complex botanical engraving/fine line-art, generous whitespace, and CTA `BUKA UNDANGAN`. CTA activation triggers a restrained sequential reveal/movement, then transitions to a separate Hero composition; normal scrolling follows. Hero must not require scrolling to appear. Target transition duration: 1.2–1.6s, validate in browser. Respect keyboard access, visible focus, semantic button behavior, and reduced motion.

## Section rules
- Hero: no photo, countdown, RSVP CTA, or long copy.
- Our Story: personal letter/editorial correspondence; actual story and format pending.
- The Wedding: main invitation card + countdown detail; exact label `UNTIL THE WEDDING`.
- Venue: engraved art is decorative only and must not claim to depict the actual venue; the factual name/address/map link remain independent. Gandrung motifs need explicit approval.
- Gallery: vertical editorial photo sequence; no generic grid or autoplay carousel.
- RSVP: only `Attending` and `Unable to Attend`. Editorial text choices with subtle visible selection indicator, not conventional-looking radio buttons. Preserve semantic single-choice behavior, keyboard access, visible focus, and state indication beyond color alone. No extra fields.
- Closing: monogram as final signature; no new decorative card system.

## Technical decisions
Frontend: Next.js + TypeScript.
RSVP backend: Supabase.
One individual per invitation link.
Sequential routes require a separate secret, unpredictable token; validate access to guest data and RSVP server-side. Token format, generation, storage, delivery, revocation and reissue remain pending before backend implementation.
RSVP is final after first submission.
Operator export access requires private login.
Administration via spreadsheet/export, not bespoke dashboard.
These decisions do not imply implementation is complete. Do not invent schema, tokens, deployment, or QA results.
