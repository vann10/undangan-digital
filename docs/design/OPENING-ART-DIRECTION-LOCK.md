## Motion and accessibility
Botanical breeze loops approximately 3.8–5 seconds; wings flap every 0.9 seconds with a slower floating path. Vary phase; keep text stationary. Pause control stops decorative loops. Reduced motion presents static decoration and immediate navigation. Decorative imagery has empty alternatives and cannot intercept pointer events. Preserve visible keyboard focus and direct Hero reveal without manual scrolling.
